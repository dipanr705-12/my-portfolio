## 👨‍💻 About The Project

Welcome to my personal portfolio website! I am **Dipan Roy**, a B.Tech student in **Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE)** from Kolkata, India. This portfolio bridges my passion for **hardware engineering, PCB design, embedded systems, and modern web development**.

### ✨ Key Highlights
- ⚡ **Interactive Pikachu Code-Thunderbolt:** Click on the Pikachu character in the Hero section to trigger a 60fps anime Thunderbolt animation where electric arcs and vortexes are formed out of real coding-language tokens (`c++`, `js`, `html`, `css`, `py`, `pcb`, `kicad`, `wokwi`).
- 📄 **Instant CV Download:** One-click PDF resume generator built directly into the navigation bar.
- 🛠️ **Structured Technical Skills Showcase:** Organized across Programming Languages, PCB Design Software, and Hardware Simulation tools.
- 📬 **Direct Contact Portal:** Integrated contact modal to connect with me directly for collaborations, internships, and projects.
